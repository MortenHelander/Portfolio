## Tough week

Week 6 has been quite difficult for me in relation to my project. I've had a hard time wrapping my head how making my project a REST API fits my project, I think honestly I might have chosen another project if I understood better what REST was when we started. My teacher told me it's completely fine on my project, although a bit forced maybe, but it shouldn't be an issue. The reason I've been struggling a bit with how I should proceed is that, REST is supposed to be stateless and my API a chess game is very much something that has a state. It feels a bit like two parralel directions when trying to keep something RESTful and at the same time trying to make a smooth online game.

Although I've been a bit demotivated compared to the previous weeks, the subject matter itself is very interesting and doesn't seem to complicated. Learning about logging seemed a bit boring and trivial at first, but when being introduced to it's usefulness those thoughts were gone completely. Exceptionhandling is something I really hope to understand better along the way. We learned about global exceptionhandlers and they seem almost too good to be true. Where I'm struggling a bit however, is where I should handle the exceptions, I'm seeing a lot of mixed opinions on the internet and from what we've learned. Catching them where they're thrown seems optimal, but also like a lot of work and potential headache for refactoring later (I'm thinking the try / catch / finally).

I haven't been adding as much on my project compared to the other weeks, I finally finished testing most of my game-engine, coverage is on 85% on the game engine, and I've been adding some exception handlers and endpoints.
Next week I hope I can see how my project makes sense in relation to REST otherwise I'll have to accept that this is just a school project and at least now I have a better understanding of all the concepts, even though my project might not be as cool as I hoped for.
